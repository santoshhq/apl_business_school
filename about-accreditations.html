Accreditations & Affiliations

Quality Assured Education Through Trusted Partnerships

Quality Assurance and Recognition

At ALP Business School, we are committed to delivering qualifications that are recognised, respected, and valued by employers across the UK and internationally. Our programmes are delivered in partnership with established UK awarding organisations that are regulated by Ofqual (Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation) and other UK regulatory bodies.

All qualifications offered through ALP Business School are aligned with the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) and meet stringent quality standards. This ensures that our students receive credentials that are widely recognised and accepted by employers, professional bodies, and higher education institutions.

We undergo regular quality reviews and audits to ensure that our teaching, assessment, and administrative processes meet the highest standards of academic integrity and professional practice.

Our Awarding Organisation Partners

ALP Business School works in collaboration with reputable UK awarding organisations to deliver qualifications across our programme portfolio. These partnerships ensure that our qualifications meet rigorous quality standards and are recognised by employers and higher education institutions.

Our Quality Standards

ALP Business School adheres to comprehensive quality assurance frameworks to ensure that every aspect of our provision meets or exceeds regulatory requirements and best practices in the education sector.

We maintain quality through:

  • Regular External Quality Assurance: Independent verification of our teaching, assessment, and administrative processes by awarding organisations
  • Rigorous Internal Quality Assurance: Systematic monitoring and evaluation of programme delivery, student outcomes, and support services
  • Continuous Professional Development: Ongoing training and development for all teaching and support staff to maintain current expertise
  • Student Feedback Systems: Regular collection and analysis of student feedback to drive improvement in our services
  • External Examiner Systems: Independent assessment of student work to ensure consistency and fairness in marking
  • Annual Programme Reviews: Comprehensive reviews of curriculum content, delivery methods, and student outcomes
  • Compliance Monitoring: Regular audits to ensure adherence to all regulatory requirements and awarding body standards
  • Industry Engagement: Collaboration with employers and professional bodies to ensure curriculum relevance and employability focus

Professional Affiliations and Memberships

ALP Business School maintains affiliations with various professional bodies and industry organisations to ensure our programmes remain relevant, current, and aligned with industry standards. These partnerships also provide additional benefits and opportunities for our students.

Benefits of our professional affiliations:

  • Students may be eligible for student membership with professional bodies
  • Access to professional development resources and industry publications
  • Networking opportunities with industry professionals
  • Enhanced employability through recognition by professional organisations
  • Potential for exemptions or fast-track routes to professional qualifications
  • Invitations to industry events, conferences, and workshops
