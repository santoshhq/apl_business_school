Equality and Diversity

Creating an Inclusive Learning Environment for All

Our Commitment to Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion

At ALP Business School, we are firmly committed to fostering an inclusive learning environment where every individual is valued, respected, and given equal opportunities to succeed. We believe that diversity enriches our educational community and enhances the learning experience for all our students and staff.

Our commitment to equality and diversity is not simply a legal obligation—it is a fundamental value that underpins everything we do. We actively promote an institutional culture that celebrates difference, challenges discrimination, and ensures that all members of our community can achieve their full potential regardless of their background or characteristics.

We are committed to complying with the Equality Act 2010 and all relevant UK legislation relating to equality, diversity, and human rights. Beyond legal compliance, we strive to create a positive, welcoming environment where everyone feels they belong and can contribute meaningfully to our academic community.

Protected Characteristics

In accordance with the Equality Act 2010, ALP Business School does not discriminate against any individual on the basis of the following protected characteristics:

Age
Disability
Gender Reassignment
Marriage & Civil Partnership
Pregnancy & Maternity
Race

We ensure that our admissions, teaching, assessment, and support services are fair, transparent, and accessible to all, regardless of these characteristics.

Our Key Principles

Equal Opportunities

We provide equal access to educational opportunities and ensure that all students receive fair treatment throughout their studies with us.

Inclusive Culture

We foster a culture where diversity is celebrated and all individuals feel welcomed, valued, and able to participate fully.

Zero Tolerance

We have zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind and take prompt action to address any concerns.

Reasonable Adjustments

We make appropriate reasonable adjustments to support students with disabilities or additional learning needs.

Fair Assessment

Our assessment practices are fair, valid, and free from bias, ensuring that all students are evaluated on their academic merit.

Staff Development

We provide equality and diversity training for all staff to ensure they understand and uphold our inclusive values.

Our Specific Commitments

To demonstrate our commitment to equality and diversity, ALP Business School will:

  • Ensure that our admissions processes are fair, transparent, and accessible, with decisions based solely on academic merit and potential.
  • Provide accessible learning materials and resources, including options for students with visual or hearing impairments.
  • Make reasonable adjustments to teaching, assessment, and support services for students with disabilities or additional needs.
  • Create an inclusive curriculum that reflects diverse perspectives and prepares students to work in multicultural environments.
  • Provide dedicated support for students who may face barriers to learning, including financial hardship, caring responsibilities, or prior educational disadvantage.
  • Monitor our student population and outcomes to identify and address any equality gaps in achievement or retention.
  • Take prompt and appropriate action to investigate and resolve any complaints of discrimination, harassment, or victimisation.
  • Regularly review and update our policies and practices to ensure they promote equality and remove barriers to participation.
  • Celebrate diversity through our communications, marketing materials, and institutional culture.
  • Work in partnership with external organisations to promote equality and support under-represented groups in higher education.

Support for Students with Disabilities

ALP Business School is committed to ensuring that students with disabilities and learning differences have equal opportunities to participate fully in their education and achieve their academic goals. We recognise that disability can take many forms, including physical disabilities, sensory impairments, mental health conditions, and specific learning difficulties such as dyslexia or dyspraxia.

We will work closely with students to understand their individual needs and implement appropriate reasonable adjustments. These may include:

  • Extended deadlines for coursework and assessments
  • Alternative assessment formats or methods
  • Additional time in examinations
  • Accessible learning materials in alternative formats
  • Assistive technology and specialist software
  • One-to-one support from specialist tutors
  • Flexible attendance arrangements for online or on-campus sessions
  • Note-taking support or lecture recordings

Students are encouraged to disclose any disability or additional needs during the application process or at any point during their studies. All information is treated confidentially and used solely to provide appropriate support.

Reporting Concerns and Seeking Support

If you experience or witness any form of discrimination, harassment, or unfair treatment at ALP Business School, we encourage you to report it. We take all complaints seriously and will investigate them promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

You can raise concerns through our formal complaints procedure or by speaking directly to your tutor, programme leader, or our student support team. We will ensure that anyone who raises a legitimate concern in good faith will not face any detriment or victimisation as a result.

Our goal is to resolve issues quickly and effectively whilst ensuring that appropriate lessons are learnt to prevent similar incidents in the future.
