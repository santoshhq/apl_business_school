Level 6

Business & Administrative Management
(Level 6)

Designed for experienced professionals with managerial experience who wish to progress to an MBA or postgraduate business programme.

About This Course

The level 6 diploma in business administration is made up of 10 modules and 5 written assignments; each module consists of approximately 40 guided learning hours. While completing the online business diploma, students will have access to various study resources, such as tutor support and webinars, a social learning forum, and additional materials to help in the completion of their graduate business course.

This course will be most suited to individuals who are at least 25 years old and have 5 years managerial work experience, and do not have a degree but wish to study for an MBA or postgraduate business programme. On successful completion of the course, students will be able to enrol onto our level 7 postgraduate diploma in strategic management and leadership or a range of other postgraduate courses.

Entry Requirements

This course is most suited to individuals who are at least 25 years old and have a minimum of 5 years' managerial work experience and do not currently hold a degree qualification.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Strategic business administration and management
  • Advanced leadership and organisational behaviour
  • Business planning and corporate strategy
  • Financial management and business performance
  • Change management and innovation
  • Professional ethics and governance

Progression Options

On successful completion of this course, students can progress to:

  • Level 7 Postgraduate Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership
  • A range of other MBA and postgraduate business programmes
