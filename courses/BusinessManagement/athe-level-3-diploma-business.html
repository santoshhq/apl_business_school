ATHE Level 3 Diploma in Business
(60 Credits)

An Ofqual-regulated qualification equivalent to an A Level, designed for learners aspiring to progress to university-level study in business and management.

About This Course

The ATHE Level 3 Diploma in Business is a 60-credit Ofqual-regulated qualification, equivalent to an A Level or Access to Higher Education qualification. It is designed for learners aspiring to progress to university-level study in business, management, or related fields but who may not hold traditional academic entry qualifications.

This diploma provides a strong foundation in key business concepts, including marketing, finance, management, communication, and organisational behaviour, helping learners develop both theoretical understanding and practical business skills. The course also enhances critical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities essential for success in higher education and professional settings.

What You'll Learn

  • Marketing principles and consumer behaviour
  • Financial management and business accounting
  • Business management and organisational structures
  • Effective business communication strategies
  • Organisational behaviour and team dynamics
  • Critical thinking and analytical reasoning
  • Problem-solving and decision-making techniques
  • Academic study skills for higher education

Who Is This Course For?

This qualification is ideal for:

  • Learners without traditional A-level qualifications who want to access university
  • Individuals seeking a career change into business and management
  • Those wanting to strengthen their business knowledge before higher education
  • Working professionals looking to formalize their business experience
  • Anyone interested in developing a solid foundation in business principles

Course Structure

The ATHE Level 3 Diploma consists of carefully designed units totaling 60 credits. Each unit covers essential business topics with a blend of theoretical concepts and practical applications. Assessment is through written assignments that allow you to demonstrate your understanding and apply business principles to real-world scenarios.

Assessment Methods

All units are assessed through written assignments that test your knowledge, understanding, and ability to apply business concepts. You'll receive comprehensive feedback from experienced tutors to support your learning and development throughout the course.

Progression Opportunities

Upon successful completion, you can progress to:

  • ATHE Level 4 Extended Diploma in Management
  • University undergraduate degrees in business, management, and related fields
  • Foundation year programmes at UK universities
  • Direct employment in business administration and management roles
  • Further professional qualifications in business and management

Why Choose This Qualification?

  • Ofqual-regulated and nationally recognized
  • Equivalent to an A Level qualification
  • Flexible distance learning with full tutor support
  • No examinations – assessed entirely through assignments
  • Excellent pathway to university and professional careers
  • Develop essential academic and workplace skills
