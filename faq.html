Your Questions Answered

Find answers to the most common questions about our courses, qualifications, and enrollment process.

ALP Business School offers Ofqual-regulated qualifications ranging from Level 3 to Level 7. Our portfolio includes Business Management, Human Resource Management, Administrative Management, Teacher Training, and Introduction to Management courses. All qualifications are nationally recognized and designed to help you advance your career or progress to university.

Yes! All our courses are delivered 100% online, allowing you to study from anywhere in the world at your own pace. You'll have access to comprehensive learning materials, video lectures, interactive assignments, and dedicated tutor support throughout your studies. This flexible approach means you can balance your education with work and personal commitments.

Course duration varies depending on the level and your study pace. Typically, Level 3 courses take 6-9 months, Level 4 & 5 combined diplomas take 12-18 months, and Level 6 courses take 9-12 months. However, as our courses are self-paced, you can complete them faster if you dedicate more time, or take longer if needed.

Entry requirements vary by level. For Level 3 courses, you typically need basic English proficiency and be 18 years or older. Level 4 & 5 courses generally require a Level 3 qualification or equivalent work experience. Level 6 courses require a Level 5 qualification or substantial management experience. We assess each application individually to ensure you're placed on the right course for your background and goals.

Absolutely! Our Level 4 & 5 diplomas are equivalent to the first two years of a UK undergraduate degree, allowing you to progress directly into the final year (top-up) of a relevant bachelor's degree at many UK universities. Level 6 qualifications are equivalent to a bachelor's degree and can lead to postgraduate studies. We provide guidance on university progression pathways throughout your course.

You'll receive comprehensive support throughout your journey with ALP Business School. This includes a dedicated personal tutor who provides feedback on your assignments, academic guidance, and career advice. You'll also have access to our student support team for administrative queries, online learning resources, webinars, and a student community forum where you can connect with fellow learners.

Course fees vary depending on the level and program you choose. We offer competitive pricing compared to traditional education providers. Yes, we offer flexible payment plans to make your education more affordable. You can pay in monthly installments, and we also accept various payment methods. Contact our admissions team for specific pricing and to discuss payment options that suit your budget.

Yes, all our qualifications are regulated by Ofqual (the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation) and are nationally recognized in the UK. They hold the same value as qualifications from traditional educational institutions and are widely respected by employers across various industries. Many of our graduates have successfully used their ALP qualifications to advance their careers, secure promotions, or transition into new roles.

Yes! Our online courses are accessible worldwide, and we welcome international students. As long as you have a reliable internet connection and meet the English language requirements, you can study from anywhere. Our qualifications are recognized internationally, and many of our students study from countries across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East while working full-time in their home countries.

Applying is easy! Simply visit our Apply Now page and complete the online application form. You'll need to provide some basic personal information, educational background, and your preferred course. Our admissions team will review your application within 2-3 business days. We offer flexible start dates throughout the year, so you can typically begin your studies within 2-4 weeks of your application being approved.
